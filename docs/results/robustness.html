    CBIS-DDSM settings#

    The same pattern appears in every CBIS-DDSM setting:

    • distance correlation and both fixes shrink the gap;
    • CAM regularization and both fixes cut off-breast attention;
    • CAM regularization does not shrink the gap;
    • distance correlation does not cut off-breast attention.

    Things to note:

    • ResNet-50: the effect is weaker; both fixes shrink the gap in 3 of 5 runs.
    • DenseNet-121: both fixes cost 0.080 AUROC, without retuning the weights.
    • Clinical split: CAM regularization slightly increases the gap (+0.079).

    INbreast (digital mammograms)#

    • Distance correlation and both fixes again shrink the gap (−0.218 and −0.171, in 11 and 10 of 13 runs).
    • CAM regularization again removes off-breast attention (−0.439) without shrinking the gap (+0.031).
    • One difference: on INbreast, distance correlation also lowers SAS (−0.126).

    Treat INbreast as supporting evidence

    On INbreast even the baseline struggles (AUROC 0.574, gap 0.189 ± 0.369), and each training set keeps only about 8 cancers. The direction matches CBIS-DDSM, but this is a small check, not a full repeat.
