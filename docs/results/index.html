    Results

    Results overview

    Key findings#

    • Distance correlation shrinks the group gap from 0.357 to 0.083, but the heat maps still spill outside the breast (SAS goes up slightly, by 0.093).
    • CAM regularization cuts off-breast attention from 0.557 to 0.018, but the group gap barely moves (0.331).
    • Both fixes together give a gap of 0.065 and SAS of 0.021, for about two AUROC points (0.705 → 0.684).
    • Gradient reversal with default settings makes things worse (AUROC 0.549, gap 0.664). With better settings it shrinks the gap about as well as distance correlation.
    • No published method tested moves SAS by more than 0.09.
    • These patterns hold with weaker shortcuts, other backbones, the clinical density split, many penalty weights, and, in direction, on INbreast.
